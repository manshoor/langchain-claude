Customer support bot on Claude: LangChain, LangGraph, Langfuse

The repo builds a support bot that answers from 11 policy documents, cites its sources, remembers each customer and hands off to a human when the documents have no answer. It is built twice: once as one agent (05), once as a LangGraph workflow where three Claude models split each turn (06). Both have guardrails against prompt injection. Evals measure both.
