# Warranty (sample data)

All hardware comes with a 2-year manufacturer warranty from the date of delivery.

The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship. It does not cover accidental damage, water damage, or normal wear.

To make a claim, send the serial number and a photo or video of the fault to support. We repair or replace the device within 10 business days.

An extended 3-year warranty can be bought within 30 days of purchase for 149 AED.
