# Account security (sample data)

Passwords must be at least 12 characters long.

Two-factor authentication (2FA) can be turned on with an authenticator app or SMS codes. It is mandatory for Team and Enterprise admins.

After 5 failed sign-in attempts the account locks for 15 minutes.

If you lose access to your 2FA device, contact support with photo ID to reset it. Resets take up to 2 business days.
