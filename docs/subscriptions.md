# Subscriptions and cancellation (sample data)

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each monthly or yearly billing period.

You can cancel at any time from Settings > Billing. Access continues until the end of the period you already paid for.

Monthly plans are not prorated. Yearly plans cancelled within the first 14 days get a full refund; after that, no partial refunds are given.

We email a reminder 7 days before a yearly plan renews.
