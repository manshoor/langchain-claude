# Shipping and delivery (sample data)

Orders inside the UAE ship within 2 business days and arrive in 1 to 3 days.

GCC orders (Saudi Arabia, Oman, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait) arrive in 4 to 7 days.

Shipping is free on orders over 200 AED. Smaller orders pay a flat 25 AED fee.

Every order gets a tracking link by SMS and email once it leaves the warehouse.

We do not ship to PO boxes.
