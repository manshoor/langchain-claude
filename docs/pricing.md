# Plans and pricing (sample data)

Starter costs 49 AED per month and includes 1 user and 10 GB of storage.

Team costs 199 AED per month and includes up to 10 users and 1 TB of storage.

Enterprise pricing is quoted per contract and includes single sign-on, audit logs and a dedicated account manager.

Yearly billing gives two months free on Starter and Team.

All prices exclude 5% UAE VAT.
